SOURCE: Nutritional Strategies for Basketball Performance (2022)
ORGANIZATION: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition (JISSN)
CATEGORY: Basketball - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
Basketball requires short bursts of maximal energy, relying heavily on muscle glycogen. Proper fueling strategies are needed to sustain performance across four quarters and minimize fatigue late in the game.

DAILY MACRONUTRIENT RECOMMENDATIONS
- Carbohydrates: 5-7 g/kg body weight to replenish glycogen stores during heavy training or multi-game weeks.
- Protein: 1.4-1.7 g/kg body weight to support muscle recovery and maintenance, particularly important given the physical contact and eccentric loading (landing) in the sport.
- Fats: 20-35% of total caloric intake, focusing on healthy sources (avocados, nuts, olive oil) for general health and hormone production.

PRE-GAME FUELING
- Consume a meal high in carbohydrates (1-4 g/kg), moderate in protein, and low in fat/fiber 3-4 hours before tip-off.
- Example: Grilled chicken breast, large portion of pasta with tomato sauce, side salad.
- A small, easy-to-digest carbohydrate snack (e.g., banana, sports gel) can be consumed 30-60 minutes before the game.

IN-GAME NUTRITION
- Muscle glycogen depletion is a primary cause of fatigue in the 4th quarter.
- Players should aim to consume 30-60g of carbohydrates per hour during the game, primarily through sports drinks during timeouts and halftime.

POST-GAME RECOVERY
- Target the 4 R's: Refuel, Rebuild, Rehydrate, Rest.
- Consume a recovery shake or meal containing carbohydrates and protein (approx. 3:1 or 4:1 ratio) within 45 minutes of the game ending.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Carbohydrates are the critical fuel for high-intensity basketball performance.
- Consume a carb-heavy meal 3-4 hours before the game.
- Use sports drinks during the game to maintain energy levels in the second half.
