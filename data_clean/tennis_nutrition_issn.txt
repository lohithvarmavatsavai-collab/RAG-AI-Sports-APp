SOURCE: Fueling for Tennis - Carbohydrate and Fluid Strategies (2022)
ORGANIZATION: International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN)
CATEGORY: Tennis - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
The ISSN highlights the critical role of carbohydrate availability and fluid balance in racquet sports like tennis. Fatigue in tennis is often caused by glycogen depletion or dehydration, both of which impair stroke accuracy, footwork, and decision-making.

CARBOHYDRATE STRATEGIES
- Match-day fueling: Players should aim for a high-carbohydrate meal (2-3 g/kg) roughly 3 hours before the match.
- Intra-match fueling: For matches lasting longer than 1 hour, consuming 30-60 grams of carbohydrates per hour is recommended to maintain blood glucose levels.
- Practical sources: Sports drinks (which also provide hydration), energy chews, and ripe bananas are excellent choices during changeovers.

HYDRATION AND ELECTROLYTES
- Sweat rates in tennis can exceed 1.5-2.0 Liters per hour in hot conditions.
- Sodium is the primary electrolyte lost in sweat. Tennis players are prone to cramping if sodium is not replaced.
- Fluid intake during matches should match sweat loss, typically equating to 150-250 ml (5-8 oz) every 15-minute changeover.

TOURNAMENT CONSIDERATIONS
- In tournaments with multiple matches per day, immediate post-match refueling is critical.
- Players should consume 1.0-1.2 g/kg of carbohydrates along with 20g of protein immediately after their first match.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Maintain blood sugar during play with 30-60g carbs/hour.
- Replace fluids and sodium at every changeover to prevent cramping and fatigue.
- Rapid refueling is essential during multi-match tournament days.
