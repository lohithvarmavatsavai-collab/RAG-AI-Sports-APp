SOURCE: NCAA Basketball - Sports Science and Performance Nutrition Guidelines (2023)
ORGANIZATION: National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Sport Science Institute
CATEGORY: Basketball - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
The NCAA Sport Science Institute provides evidence-based nutrition education for collegiate athletes. Basketball players — particularly those transitioning from high school to college — frequently have poor nutritional habits. This document addresses caloric needs, fueling timing, and the unique challenges of the basketball schedule.

CALORIC AND MACRONUTRIENT NEEDS BY POSITION
- Guards (high movement): 3,000–3,800 kcal/day during season.
- Forwards/Centers (strength-dominant): 3,500–4,500 kcal/day during season, higher during pre-season conditioning.
- Carbohydrates: 5–7 g/kg/day during season. 7–10 g/kg/day on game days.
- Protein: 1.6–2.0 g/kg/day to support muscle maintenance throughout the long season.
- Fat: 25–35% of total calories. Prioritize unsaturated fats.

PRE-GAME NUTRITION PROTOCOL (NCAA Standard)
- 3–4 hours before tipoff: Full meal. High carbohydrate (1.5–3 g/kg), moderate protein, low fat. Examples: turkey sandwich on whole grain, pasta with chicken and marinara, rice bowl with lean protein.
- 1–2 hours before: Light carbohydrate snack only (banana, granola bar, small sports drink). Avoid high fat or high fiber at this point.
- 30 minutes before: Optional small carbohydrate snack (energy gel, sports chews, half banana).

HALFTIME AND IN-GAME FUELING
- Use the halftime break (15 min) to:
 - Drink 12–20 oz of sports drink or water.
 - Eat a small carbohydrate snack if needed (banana, energy chews) for games lasting over 90 minutes of actual playing time.
- During timeouts: sips of fluid only.

POST-GAME RECOVERY NUTRITION
- The NCAA recommends teams have a recovery protocol in place — ideally a team meal within 2 hours post-game.
- Target: 1.0–1.2 g/kg carbohydrates + 0.3 g/kg protein (approximately 20–25 g for most players) within the first hour.
- Chocolate milk has been validated in multiple studies as an effective recovery drink (provides ~4:1 carb:protein ratio, plus fluids and electrolytes).

TRAVEL NUTRITION
- College teams travel extensively. Key principles:
 - Pack portable nutrient-dense snacks (nuts, nut butter packets, protein bars with < 10 g sugar, jerky).
 - Avoid greasy/fast food on travel days — GI distress is a major performance risk.
 - Prioritize sleep and hydration during air travel.

WEIGHT AND BODY COMPOSITION
- Do NOT try to lose weight during the competitive season.
- Pre-season is the appropriate time for body composition changes, with dietitian oversight.
- Minimum body fat recommendations: Males: ≥ 5%; Females: ≥ 12%.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Game-day carbohydrate target: 7–10 g/kg total.
- Pre-game meal 3–4 hours prior: High carbohydrate, moderate protein, low fat/fiber.
- Chocolate milk is an excellent, accessible post-game recovery option.
- Never attempt weight loss during the competitive season.
