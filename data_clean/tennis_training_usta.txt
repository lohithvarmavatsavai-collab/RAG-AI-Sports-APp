SOURCE: USTA High Performance Tennis Conditioning Program (2022)
ORGANIZATION: United States Tennis Association (USTA)
CATEGORY: Tennis - Training
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
The USTA emphasizes that tennis conditioning must mimic the work-to-rest ratios of actual match play. A typical point lasts 5-10 seconds, followed by 20-25 seconds of rest between points, and 90 seconds between changeovers.

ON-COURT CONDITIONING
- Spider Drill: Sprinting to 5 different points on the court and returning to the center. Develops multi-directional speed.
- Suicides (Lines): Sprinting to the service line, baseline, and back. Focuses on acceleration and deceleration.
- Work-to-Rest Ratio: Drills should use a 1:2 or 1:3 work-to-rest ratio to simulate match conditions. Example: 10 seconds of sprinting, 20-30 seconds of rest.

STRENGTH TRAINING FOCUS
- Lower Body: Squats, lunges, lateral bounds. The power for strokes originates in the legs.
- Core: Medicine ball throws (rotational power), cable woodchoppers, heavy carries.
- Upper Body: Pull-ups, rows, push-ups. Pulling exercises should be prioritized to balance the heavy pushing/internal rotation of serving and hitting forehands.

PLYOMETRICS
- Box jumps, medicine ball slams, and lateral skater jumps improve explosive power.
- Beginners should start with low-intensity plyometrics (e.g., jump rope, basic bounding) before progressing to high-impact jumps.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Condition on the court using tennis-specific movements.
- Match the work-to-rest ratios of real matches.
- Build lower body power for better court coverage and stroke velocity.
