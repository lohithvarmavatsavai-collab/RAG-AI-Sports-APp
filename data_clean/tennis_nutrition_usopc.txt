SOURCE: Nutritional Periodization and Weight Management for Competitive Tennis Players (2022)
ORGANIZATION: United States Olympic and Paralympic Committee (USOPC) / USTA High Performance
CATEGORY: Tennis - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
Competitive tennis presents unique nutritional challenges: variable match duration (1 to 5+ hours), extreme heat, back-to-back tournament scheduling, and wide variation in physical demands across surfaces (hard, clay, grass). This USOPC-aligned guide addresses periodized nutrition for competitive club and tournament tennis.

ENERGY REQUIREMENTS
- Male competitive players: 2,800–4,000 kcal/day depending on training volume and match load.
- Female competitive players: 2,200–3,200 kcal/day.
- During heavy tournament weeks (3+ matches): Increase total caloric intake by 300–500 kcal/day to avoid energy deficit.

CARBOHYDRATE PERIODIZATION FOR TENNIS
- Pre-tournament (2 days before): Increase carbohydrates to 8–10 g/kg/day (mini-carbohydrate load).
- Match day (short match, <90 min): 5–6 g/kg carbohydrates total for the day.
- Match day (long match, >90 min): 7–10 g/kg. Include intra-match carbohydrates (30–60 g/hr via sports drinks, gels, or banana pieces during changeovers).
- Rest days: 3–5 g/kg. Focus on protein and vegetables.

PROTEIN FOR TENNIS PLAYERS
- Daily: 1.5–1.8 g/kg body weight.
- During heavy tournament play: Up to 2.0 g/kg to support muscle repair when recovery time between matches is short.
- Best sources: Chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey/plant protein.

HEAT NUTRITION STRATEGIES (USOPC Guidelines)
- Hot conditions increase carbohydrate oxidation rate by up to 15%.
- Sodium intake becomes critical — muscle cramps in heat are driven primarily by sodium loss, not just fluid loss.
- Pre-event sodium loading: Add 1,000–1,500 mg of sodium in the pre-match meal (e.g., sports drink, salted pretzels, broth).
- Cool drinks are absorbed faster than warm drinks — use ice-cold beverages when possible in heat.

WEIGHT MANAGEMENT IN TENNIS
- Tennis players sometimes attempt to cut weight for speed gains. This is risky:
 - Rapid weight loss impairs reaction time, power output, and concentration.
 - If weight loss is appropriate (gradual, off-season), aim for no more than 0.5 kg/week loss while keeping protein at 2.0 g/kg to protect muscle mass.
 - Never cut weight during a tournament or heavy training block.

PRACTICAL MATCH-DAY NUTRITION PLAN
- 3–4 hours before match: Chicken and rice bowl (300 g cooked rice, 150 g chicken, olive oil, banana). 600 mL water.
- 60 minutes before: 1 banana or 1 energy gel + 300 mL sports drink.
- During match (changeovers): Sips of sports drink (150–200 mL every changeover). At set break: half a banana or energy gel.
- Post-match (within 30 min): Recovery shake (30 g protein + 60 g fast carb) or chocolate milk (500 mL).
- Post-match meal (within 2 hours): Grilled salmon, sweet potato, green vegetables.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Carbohydrate load 2 days before tournaments.
- Maintain 30–60 g/hr of carbohydrates during matches longer than 90 minutes.
- Sodium is as critical as fluids in hot conditions — salt your pre-match meal.
- Never attempt weight loss during competition periods.
