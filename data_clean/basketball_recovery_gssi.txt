SOURCE: Hydration Monitoring and Recovery for Basketball Players (2022)
ORGANIZATION: Gatorade Sports Science Institute (GSSI)
CATEGORY: Basketball - Recovery
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
GSSI focuses on precise hydration monitoring and post-game recovery tactics for basketball players, recognizing that indoor stadium conditions can still lead to substantial fluid loss.

HYDRATION MONITORING
- Urine color charts should be posted in locker rooms. Urine should be pale yellow.
- Frequent cramping is often a sign of sodium depletion, not just fluid loss. Players prone to cramping should add a sodium supplement or salty snacks to their pre-game routine.

RECOVERY NUTRITION TIMING
- The "Anabolic Window": Consuming a recovery meal or shake within 30-60 minutes post-game maximizes glycogen replenishment and initiates muscle repair.
- The recovery meal should contain approx. 1.0-1.2 g/kg of carbohydrates and 20-25g of high-quality protein (e.g., whey, casein, or a soy/pea blend).

TRAVEL CONSIDERATIONS
- Air travel dehydrates the body. Players should drink extra water during flights.
- Traveling across time zones disrupts circadian rhythms, making sleep management the primary recovery focus during road trips.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Monitor hydration daily using urine color.
- Address cramping with both fluids and sodium.
- Capitalize on the 30-60 minute post-game recovery window with carbs and protein.
