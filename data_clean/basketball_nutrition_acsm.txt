SOURCE: Game-day Hydration and Fueling Protocol for Basketball (2021)
ORGANIZATION: American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)
CATEGORY: Basketball - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
The ACSM provides strict fluid replacement guidelines to prevent dehydration, which severely impacts shooting accuracy and defensive positioning in basketball.

HYDRATION BEFORE THE GAME
- Drink 5-7 ml/kg of body weight of water or sports drink roughly 4 hours prior to tip-off.
- If urine is dark or nonexistent, drink an additional 3-5 ml/kg about 2 hours before the game.

HYDRATION DURING THE GAME
- Basketball is played in climate-controlled environments, but high intensity and heavy uniforms lead to significant sweat rates (1-2 Liters per hour).
- Players should drink 150-250 ml of fluid during every timeout and quarter break.
- For games where players log high minutes (over 20 minutes of actual playing time), a sports drink containing 6-8% carbohydrates and electrolytes (sodium/potassium) is recommended over plain water.

REHYDRATION AFTER THE GAME
- Players should weigh themselves before and after the game to determine fluid loss.
- Drink 1.5 Liters of fluid for every 1 kg of body weight lost.
- This fluid should be consumed gradually over the next 2-4 hours, not chugged immediately.
- Consuming salty snacks or a sports drink will help the body retain the fluid.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Pre-hydrate 4 hours before the game.
- Use timeouts to maintain fluid levels.
- Rehydrate aggressively based on weight lost during the game.
