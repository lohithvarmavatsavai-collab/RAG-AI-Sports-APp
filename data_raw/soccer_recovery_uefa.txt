SOURCE: UEFA Champions League - Recovery Science and Match Congestion Protocols (2023)
ORGANIZATION: Union of European Football Associations (UEFA)
CATEGORY: Soccer - Recovery
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
UEFA's research on elite club football reveals that match congestion — playing more than one match per week — is the primary recovery challenge for modern soccer players. This document summarizes UEFA's evidence-based protocols for managing congestion and optimizing recovery, adapted for amateur and club-level athletes.

PHYSIOLOGICAL CONSEQUENCES OF A 90-MINUTE MATCH
- Muscle glycogen depleted by 40–90% depending on position.
- 1–3% body mass lost through sweat.
- Elevated creatine kinase (CK) and myoglobin (indicators of muscle damage) for 24–72 hours post-match.
- Perception of muscle soreness peaks at 24–48 hours post-match (DOMS).
- Neuromuscular function (sprint speed, jump height) reduced for 48–72 hours.

THE UEFA RECOVERY TIMELINE
- 0–30 minutes post-match: Begin refueling. Carbohydrate + protein snack. Start rehydration.
- 30 minutes – 2 hours: Full recovery meal. Cold water immersion if available.
- 2–24 hours: Sleep is the primary recovery tool. Target 9 hours.
- 24–48 hours: Active recovery sessions only. No high-intensity training.
- 48–72 hours: Light technical work. Gradual reintroduction of physical load.
- 72+ hours: Return to full training only if wellness markers are positive.

MATCH CONGESTION MANAGEMENT
- When 2 matches occur within 72 hours: Eliminate all high-intensity training sessions between matches. Prioritize sleep, nutrition, and cold water immersion.
- When 3 matches occur within 10 days: Reduce training volume by 60% on non-match days. Include at least one complete rest day.

COLD WATER IMMERSION (CWI) PROTOCOLS
- UEFA-aligned CWI: 10–12 minutes at 10–15°C within 30 minutes of full-time whistle.
- Contrast bathing (alternating warm/cold): 1 minute cold, 1 minute warm, repeated 4–5 cycles.
- CWI significantly reduces perceived fatigue and CK levels at 24 and 48 hours.

SLEEP EXTENSION PROTOCOL
- Napping 20–30 minutes at 2 PM on match days improves alertness by 30% and reaction time.
- Prioritize sleep in a cool, dark room. Core body temperature must drop to initiate deep sleep.
- Melatonin (0.5–3 mg) may help players who struggle with post-match adrenaline, but consult a physician.

NUTRITION FOR ACCELERATED RECOVERY
- 1.2 g/kg carbohydrates in the first 30–60 minutes post-match (prioritize fast-absorbing: white rice, fruit, sports drinks).
- 25–30 g protein within 2 hours (chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, or whey).
- Omega-3 fatty acids (from fish, flaxseed) reduce exercise-induced inflammation.
- Tart cherry juice (480 mL daily for 4–7 days around congested periods) — shown to reduce muscle soreness in team sport athletes.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Muscle damage and neuromuscular impairment last 48–72 hours after a match.
- The recovery window starts immediately at full-time — nutrition and CWI matter.
- Sleep 9+ hours for the two nights following a match.
- During congested fixture periods, eliminate high-intensity training between games entirely.
