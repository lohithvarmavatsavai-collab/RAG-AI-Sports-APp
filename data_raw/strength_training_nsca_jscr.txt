OVERVIEW
The NSCA publishes the most comprehensive evidence base for resistance training program design. This document synthesizes recent JSCR meta-analyses and position papers on optimal training frequency, volume, intensity, and exercise selection for beginner and intermediate lifters.

WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS ABOUT BEGINNER VS. INTERMEDIATE TRAINING
- Beginners (< 6 months consistent training): Any rep range (5–30 reps), any frequency (2–5 days/week), any resistance (60–85% 1RM) produces significant strength gains. Technique is the limiting factor, not programming.
- Intermediate (6 months – 2 years): Gains slow, requiring more specific stimulus. Must progressively overload and vary training stimulus.
- Advanced (> 2 years): Require periodized, highly individualized programs for continued progress.

OPTIMAL TRAINING FREQUENCY (JSCR Meta-Analysis, 2022)
- Muscle group frequency: Training each muscle group 2x/week produces significantly greater hypertrophy than 1x/week.
- Full-body workouts 3x/week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri) consistently outperform split routines for beginner and intermediate lifters on most hypertrophy and strength metrics.
- 4–5 days/week of training is appropriate for intermediate lifters using upper/lower splits.

VOLUME LANDMARKS (Evidence-Based)
- Minimum Effective Volume (MEV): ~4–6 sets per muscle group per week. Below this produces no meaningful growth.
- Maximum Adaptive Volume (MAV): ~12–20 sets per muscle group per week. This is the hypertrophy sweet spot.
- Maximum Recoverable Volume (MRV): > 20–25 sets. Begins to exceed recovery capacity and cause overtraining.
- Beginners should start at MEV and add 1–2 sets per week until approaching MAV.

INTENSITY AND PROGRESSION
- Strength emphasis: 3–6 reps at 80–90% 1RM.
- Hypertrophy emphasis: 8–15 reps at 65–80% 1RM.
- Muscular endurance: 15–30 reps at 40–65% 1RM.
- Linear progression (add weight each session) works effectively for beginners for the first 3–6 months.
- When linear progression stalls, switch to wave loading (e.g., Week 1: 3x8, Week 2: 3x10, Week 3: 3x12, Week 4: 3x8 heavier).

EXERCISE SELECTION PRIORITIES
- Tier 1 (Must-have): Squat, hip hinge (deadlift/RDL), vertical push (overhead press), horizontal push (bench), vertical pull (pull-up/lat pulldown), horizontal pull (row).
- Tier 2 (Important): Lunges, hip thrust, dips, face pulls, carries.
- Tier 3 (Optional): Isolation exercises (curls, lateral raises, leg extension). Add after Tier 1 and 2 are covered.

SAMPLE BEGINNER PROGRAM (3 days/week, full-body)
Day A:
- Barbell Back Squat: 3x8
- Barbell Row: 3x8
- Barbell Bench Press: 3x8
- Plank: 3x30 sec

Day B:
- Romanian Deadlift: 3x8
- Overhead Press: 3x8
- Lat Pulldown: 3x10
- Farmer's Carry: 3x20 m

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Train each muscle group 2x/week for maximum hypertrophy.
- Start with 4–6 sets per muscle group per week and build toward 12–20.
- Master Tier 1 movements before adding isolation exercises.
- Linear progression (add weight each session) is the simplest effective strategy for beginners.
