SOURCE: Nutritional Considerations for Tennis Players (2021)
ORGANIZATION: British Journal of Sports Medicine (BJSM)
CATEGORY: Tennis - Nutrition
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
Tennis poses unique nutritional challenges because match duration and environmental conditions are highly variable and unpredictable. Players must start matches fully fueled and hydrated, and have strategies to maintain energy during long matches.

MACRONUTRIENT NEEDS
1. Carbohydrates (CHO): The main fuel source.
   - Daily: 5-8 g/kg body weight depending on training load.
   - Pre-match (3-4 hours prior): 1-4 g/kg CHO. E.g., pasta, rice, bread.
   - During match: 30-60 g CHO per hour for matches exceeding 90 minutes. E.g., sports drinks, gels, bananas.
2. Protein: Essential for muscle repair.
   - Daily: 1.4-1.6 g/kg body weight.
   - Post-match: 20-25 g high-quality protein within 2 hours.
3. Fats:
   - Provide energy for lower-intensity periods but should be limited pre-match to avoid gastrointestinal (GI) distress.

PRE-MATCH NUTRITION
- A pre-match meal should be high in carbohydrates, moderate in protein, and low in fat and fiber.
- Timing: 3-4 hours before the match. A smaller snack can be consumed 1-2 hours prior.

DURING MATCH
- Small bites of banana or sips of sports drink at changeovers.
- Avoid trying new foods or supplements on match day to prevent stomach issues.

POST-MATCH RECOVERY
- The 4 R's: Refuel (carbs), Rebuild (protein), Rehydrate (fluids), and Rest.
- Consume a recovery snack (e.g., chocolate milk, fruit and yogurt) within 30-45 minutes.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Fuel with carbohydrates before and during matches over 90 minutes.
- Eat familiar, easily digestible foods on match day.
- Begin recovery fueling within 30-45 minutes post-match.
