SOURCE: ITF Physical Training Guide for Tennis (2023)
ORGANIZATION: International Tennis Federation (ITF)
CATEGORY: Tennis - Training
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
Tennis is an intermittent sport characterized by repeated high-intensity efforts interspersed with short recovery periods. The average point lasts under 10 seconds, but matches can last for hours. Training must address aerobic endurance, anaerobic power, agility, and injury prevention.

KEY FITNESS COMPONENTS
1. Aerobic Endurance: Base conditioning to sustain energy over a long match.
2. Anaerobic Power: The ability to execute explosive movements (sprints, jumps) repeatedly.
3. Agility and Change of Direction (COD): Tennis requires lateral, forward, and backward movements.
4. Strength and Power: Core strength for rotational power, shoulder/rotator cuff stability, and leg strength for groundstrokes and serves.
5. Flexibility/Mobility: Key for preventing common injuries (e.g., lower back, shoulder, knees).

TRAINING PHASES (Periodization)
- Off-Season (4-8 weeks): Focus on building aerobic base and maximal strength. High volume, lower intensity tennis play.
- Pre-Season (3-4 weeks): Shift to anaerobic power, agility, and specific tennis movements. High intensity, moderate volume.
- In-Season: Maintain fitness levels. Training volume decreases while match play increases. Focus on recovery and tactical work.
- Active Rest (1-3 weeks): Post-season recovery. Low-impact cross-training (swimming, cycling) recommended.

SAMPLE WEEKLY PLAN (Intermediate/Pre-Season)
- Monday: Tennis Practice (90 min) + Lower Body Strength (45 min)
- Tuesday: Tennis Practice (120 min) + Core & Mobility (30 min)
- Wednesday: Interval Sprints / Agility Drills (45 min)
- Thursday: Match Play (Practice match)
- Friday: Tennis Practice (90 min) + Upper Body & Rotator Cuff Strength (45 min)
- Saturday: Active Recovery (Light jog or swim)
- Sunday: Rest

INJURY PREVENTION
- The "kinetic chain" (legs to core to shoulder to arm) must be balanced.
- Shoulder Care: Perform internal/external rotation exercises with resistance bands daily.
- Core Stability: Anti-rotation exercises (e.g., Pallof press) and dynamic planks.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Train movement in all directions (multi-directional agility).
- Rotator cuff and core strength are non-negotiable for injury prevention.
- Balance aerobic endurance with high-intensity interval training (HIIT).
