SOURCE: FIFA Training Principles for Youth and Amateur Football (2023)
ORGANIZATION: FIFA - Fédération Internationale de Football Association
CATEGORY: Soccer - Training
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
FIFA's training guidelines emphasize that soccer training for beginner to intermediate players should be built around a balance of technical skill development, physical conditioning, and tactical awareness. Training should be progressive, meaning load and intensity increase gradually over weeks and months.

TRAINING PRINCIPLES
1. Frequency: Beginner players should train 2–3 sessions per week. Intermediate players can increase to 4–5 sessions, including one match.
2. Duration: Sessions should last 60–90 minutes. Youth and beginner sessions should be shorter (45–60 minutes).
3. Intensity: Training should include low, moderate, and high-intensity phases. FIFA recommends using small-sided games (SSGs) as a high-intensity training tool that also builds decision-making.
4. Recovery: At least one full rest day should follow high-intensity sessions.

PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT COMPONENTS
- Aerobic capacity: Build endurance through interval runs and continuous running. Beginners should target 70–80% max heart rate during conditioning blocks.
- Speed and agility: Include 5–10 meters of acceleration drills, direction changes, and reactive agility.
- Strength: Basic lower-body strength (squats, lunges) improves kicking power and injury prevention.
- Flexibility and mobility: Dynamic warm-ups and static cool-down stretching after every session.

SMALL-SIDED GAMES (SSG) APPROACH
SSGs (e.g., 3v3, 4v4, 5v5) are recommended for all player levels because they:
- Increase touches on the ball
- Develop tactical decision-making under pressure
- Naturally create high-intensity intervals followed by rest
- Are more engaging than isolated running drills

PERIODIZATION FOR AMATEUR PLAYERS
- Pre-season: Focus on aerobic base, technical skills
- In-season: Maintain fitness, refine tactics
- Post-season: Active recovery, reduce load by 50%

SAMPLE WEEKLY PLAN (Intermediate, 4 training days)
- Monday: Technical drill session (60 min)
- Tuesday: Aerobic interval run + strength (75 min)
- Wednesday: Rest
- Thursday: Small-sided games (75 min)
- Saturday: Match day
- Sunday: Rest or light recovery walk

INJURY PREVENTION
FIFA's 11+ warm-up program is recommended before every session for players aged 14+. It includes running, strength, and balance exercises, and has been shown to reduce injury rates by 30–50%.

KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR ATHLETES
- Progress training load gradually
- Prioritize technical skill and game intelligence alongside fitness
- Never skip warm-up and cool-down
- Rest is part of the training process, not a weakness
