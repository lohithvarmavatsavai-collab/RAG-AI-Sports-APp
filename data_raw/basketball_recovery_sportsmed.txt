SOURCE: Recovery Science in Basketball - Protocols and Best Practices (2022)
ORGANIZATION: Sports Medicine Journal
CATEGORY: Basketball - Recovery
------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
Basketball seasons are grueling, often involving 2-3 games per week and extensive travel. Recovery is essential for maintaining jump height, sprint speed, and shooting percentages across the season.

SLEEP HYGIENE
- Sleep is the most potent recovery modality.
- Players should target 8-10 hours of sleep per night.
- Napping (20-30 minutes) on game days can improve alertness, particularly when traveling across time zones.

ACTIVE RECOVERY AND MOBILITY
- The day after a game should involve light aerobic activity (e.g., stationary cycling for 15-20 minutes) to promote blood flow without adding joint stress.
- Dynamic stretching and foam rolling should target the calves, quadriceps, and IT bands, which endure heavy eccentric loading from jumping and landing.

COLD WATER IMMERSION (CWI)
- Ice baths are commonly used post-game to reduce muscle soreness and inflammation.
- Recommended protocol: 10-15°C for 10-15 minutes.
- Note: CWI should generally be avoided immediately after strength training sessions if the goal is muscle hypertrophy, but it is highly recommended after games to speed recovery for the next match.

COMPRESSION
- Wearing compression tights post-game and during travel helps reduce lower limb swelling and improves perceived recovery.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
- Prioritize 8-10 hours of sleep, supplemented by game-day naps.
- Use active recovery the day after a game.
- Cold water immersion is effective for quick turnarounds between games.
